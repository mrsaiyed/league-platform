## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-83 | What happens when a team is brought back from the bin and another team of its season now has its name | It is not brought back; the bin says so beside it and says to rename (or change the short name of) the other team on its page in Teams, then bring this one back. Why: the step's line names the clash; refusing changes nothing the admin did not ask for, and leaves the admin to choose which team keeps the name. Easy to change later (for example to offer a new name on the spot) | Small |
