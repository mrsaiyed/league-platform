| S-89 | Teams level after every tiebreaker (the coin flip, P7 §1) | Admin → Standings and stats names every set of teams level after every tiebreaker. The admin records the order a coin flip at the table gave them, place by place, or presses "Flip for us" and the server picks the order fairly at random; a short note is optional. The order can be changed (recorded again) or taken off with a reason, and the activity log keeps each. A recorded flip counts even when the season's tiebreakers do not list the coin flip: it is tried last. The public and admin standings then use it, and the note under the table says a coin flip put them in that order. A flip orders teams only while exactly the set it names is level after every tiebreaker; once one pulls clear or another joins, it orders nobody and Admin lists it as no longer in use. Reason: it is the admin's call, made in the open, and as easy to take back as to make. Easy to change: one row per flip. | Small |
