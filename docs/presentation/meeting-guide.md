# Principal meeting walkthrough

Open the portable demo in Chrome/Edge. Use the reset icon before presenting. The top bar always identifies it as a concept with fictional data.

1. **Public home:** “The league gets a home that feels like Al-Hadi: school identity, upcoming games, standings, and announcements. Students and families can browse without signing in.”
2. **Student signup:** “Students apply and pay in one journey. School approval and capacity remain separate, so the paid waitlist is visible and manageable.” Complete the sample grade/checkbox and simulated checkout. No card is requested.
3. **Commissioner → Registrations:** “School staff privately review eligibility. Payment, approval, and placement are separate facts.” Review one pending record and approve it. Show the waitlist refund preview if useful.
4. **Draft room:** “One host enters picks while captains are on a call.” Pick two players, show the roster updates, and undo one. This is a rehearsal separate from the public sample teams.
5. **Schedule:** Generate a sample round robin. Explain that real dates, availability, and school constraints are decisions for the commissioner.
6. **Live scoring:** Add points, run the clock, and substitute a player. Show how time and participation follow the lineup. Production saving is planned in the database; the concept saves only in this browser.
7. **My league:** Show the applicant's personal status. Reset the demo to show the established-player scenario with upcoming-game reminders and the last-game report. Email delivery and secure account access are planned, not running.
8. **League settings:** Turn Awards off, then open the public site to see it disappear. This demonstrates configuration without building a different application for every school.

Suggested closing: “The school sets its rules and runs its games. Students get a simpler experience, and staff have one connected place to manage the season. This prototype shows the direction; our specification defines the production work behind it.”

Before committing a launch date, agree on the exact fee, payment responsibilities, refund policy, capacity, public-name format, staff permissions, game rules, and delivery scope. Do not present sample dates, names, achievements, or results as actual school records.
