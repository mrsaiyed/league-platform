## T5 — Customization engine

| T-402 | S-109's yes to "children in full" is refused by `leagues.set_setting` itself (`no-child-recorded`, said in plain words) in an adult league with no recorded child, so a hand-posted yes cannot be saved where the screen offers none. The check reads who plays and the recorded children in the transaction that writes the yes, after that transaction holds the league's row (T-403). A yes that outlives its child (binned, or a birth date corrected) is put back by the next first child: the saves take turns for one league (T-403), so the next child's save reads the league as the bin left it. No migration is needed. | Agreed (delegated) | |
