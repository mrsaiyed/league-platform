## T6 — Live stats and realtime

| T-330 | The tracker's rosters and changes of players on the device (step 2.118). A change of players (add, several numbers in one line, renumber, rename, take off) goes through the controller like a tap: timed by the server's time, checked against the contract and against the server's own rules (S-37, S-45) before anything is stored, every change of one line checked before any is stored, stored before it is listed, refused when the device is not scoring, listed as not saved on a loss, in the one queue with the person who made it (T-327). The device fetches the rosters on opening, on taking over, after a loss, after a refused change and when back online, and keeps each. The list is always the kept rosters with this device's waiting changes on top; a change the server took is laid on the kept rosters until a fetch begun after it answers. With no answer on opening the device starts from the rosters it kept before this opening, not the page's copy. An add whose answer was lost is checked against the fetched rosters before it is called not saved, and one found there leaves the list and the storage. The Players panel (part 2) draws that list and decides nothing: each player's marks and rules come from the device's rules file and every change goes through the controller. What is typed in it is kept in the browser's own storage by game, so a half-typed line survives the panel shut and a reload; losing that storage loses only typing. On an upright phone, while starters are picked, each team takes half of the teams' place and scrolls by itself, so seventeen a side never pushes the second team under the pad. | Agreed (delegated) | |
