## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-70 | When the button to score a game shows on a league's public pages | To a signed-in person who may score that game and to nobody else: on a game being played, and on a game still to play from the start of its own day in the league's time, until it is final. A game days ahead shows nothing (the menu's "My games to score" and Admin → Games lead to it). A final game that can still be reopened shows nothing: reopening is a correction with a reason and stays in Admin → Games. The words are "Score this game" for a game recorded live, also when it already has plays, and "Enter the score" for a game recorded as a final score or a box score. Why: a scorekeeper handed a phone finds the game where they look for it, and an admin does not see a button on every game of the season | A small change in one rule |
