## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-77 | What "Enter again" does on a play kept on the scorekeeper's device (one this device recorded that the league never got, or that the league refused) | It is offered only while this device is the one scoring the game. "Enter again" then records the play as a new play now, the same way as a tap (the same checks, and Undo takes it back), and takes it off the list. The clock, Undo, Redo and End game are never entered again. On a device that is not scoring the game the list has no button and says it is for reading out to whoever is. Why: the league's record must have one writer at a time, and a scorekeeper who lost the game for a minute must not lose the plays. Sending the whole list at once waits for step 2.148's server call (S-43). The offline warning (step 2.37, part 2): plays waiting with no answer for 5 minutes give one notice, "No connection for several minutes. {count} plays are only on this device until the connection comes back. Keep this page open and the device switched on: they are sent by themselves." Why 5 minutes: long enough not to nag in a gym with patchy signal, short enough that a scorekeeper hears of it within a quarter. A league's admin cannot set it yet; it is easy to make a setting later. | A small change in one screen |
