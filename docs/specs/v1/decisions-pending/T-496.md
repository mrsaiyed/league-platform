## T4 — Keeping leagues apart, sign-in, security

| T-496 | A membership's mending always works. Every `revoke <role> from <holder>` the checks print (a reached role's fault, a predefined role, the website's way to `queue_owner`) carries `cascade` when the holder made a grant of that role, which hangs on its membership. The website's way to `queue_owner` is cut at the website's end: at the first role on it that the website does not need (`app_user` and `sign_in_store` it does), so a website that reaches the worker's login, even by an ADMIN-only membership, is mended by `revoke <worker login> from <website login> cascade`, never by taking `queue_owner` from the worker. A middle role is now revoked from the website (`revoke <middle> from <website login>`), not `queue_owner` from the middle, which another login may share. Technical, decided by the lead (the fix-2 brief), built by the builder. | Agreed (delegated) | |
