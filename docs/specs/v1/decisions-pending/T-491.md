## T4 — Keeping leagues apart, sign-in, security

| T-491 | The checks ask PostgreSQL what a login CAN do (step 2.112's review, T-490, T-60). **Predefined roles.** Neither the website's login nor the worker's may be a member, at any depth and whatever the membership's options, of a role PostgreSQL made (its name begins `pg_`): each known one is listed in `predefined-roles.ts` with why (`pg_read_all_data` and `pg_write_all_data` read or write every table, the job queue's included; `pg_read_server_files`, `pg_write_server_files` and `pg_execute_server_program` reach the server's files and programs; `pg_monitor` and `pg_read_all_stats` read every session's queries; the rest each widen reach in a way listed there), and a `pg_` role not listed (a later PostgreSQL's) is refused until it is read. Only the first predefined role on a way is named, with `revoke <role> from <the role that holds it>`; while one is held the queue's and the fence's checks are not asked, since every right they read would come through it. **Effective rights on the queue.** The website's check asks `has_schema_privilege`, `has_table_privilege`, `has_any_column_privilege`, `has_sequence_privilege` and `has_function_privilege` for the login and every role it reaches (a superuser aside, named as one already), not the schema's grant list alone: any right on `pgboss`, or on a table, view, sequence or function in it, is refused. A function there counts when a reached role is granted it by name or when it is a security definer: every function may be run by everyone unless revoked, and pg-boss's own are no definers and sit in a schema the website may not use. **Mendings the owner can run.** A grant made by `queue_owner` or by an object's owner is revoked as that role: the owner grants itself the role with SET for that one statement and takes it back, as 0031 does; a membership of `queue_owner` whose holder made grants of it is revoked with `cascade`; the schema is given back to `queue_owner` in three statements. Each is run as printed by the owner on the real PostgreSQL in `tests/real-postgres/queue-review.test.ts`; the predefined roles only on PGlite, since the owner there may not grant them. | Agreed (delegated) | |
