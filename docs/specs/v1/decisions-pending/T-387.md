## T3 — Data model

| T-387 | Standings in an archived season (O-12): an adjustment, an award and an overturn always carry a reason, so they are an admin's correction with a reason and pass; a rebuild changes no fact and passes; a coin flip passes only with its note, and taking an adjustment or a coin flip off only with its reason, else each is refused season-archived. The flip's note and the removals' reason are optional elsewhere, so the one rule is: in an archived season a standings change needs its reason. The lead confirmed this against O-12 at step 2.130's review: a standings change with a reason is an admin's correction with a reason | Agreed (delegated) | |
