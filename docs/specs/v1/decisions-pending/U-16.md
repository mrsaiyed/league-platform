## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| U-16 | Which offensive words a short name may not be, and how they are matched | A starter list of 43 English words with no everyday meaning a league could want (swearing, slurs, sexual words, Nazi words), in `packages/core/src/modules/leagues/blocked-short-names.ts`. A name is refused when it is one of them or has one as a word between its hyphens (`shit-hoops`), in any capitals; a word that only holds one inside it (`grapevine-hoops`) is taken. Words offensive only in some uses (`cock`, `dyke`, `fag`, `spic`), misspellings, numbers for letters and other languages are not on it. The script that makes a league says "that word is not allowed (one of ours, or offensive). Choose another." Why: a children's league site must not be reachable at such an address, and refusing a word inside a longer name keeps the list short | Add or remove a word: one line in one file. Matching whole names only is a small change in one function |
