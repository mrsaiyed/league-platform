## T6 — Live stats and realtime

| T-453 | A scorer's waiting taps are never lost on a 404 (step 2.223). A tracker endpoint says a game is gone only with the body `{ "error": "unknown-game" }` at 404 (`TrackerRefusal` in `packages/contracts`), which the server gives only past the league gate, to a person who may open the league; the session, not-started and reopen doors say it in their own replies as before. Every other 404 from a league endpoint, the gate's `not-found` (a hidden or missing league, or a scorer signed out in a draft league), is read by the tracker (`asksSignIn` in `tracker-controller.ts`) as "sign in again" with every waiting tap held, exactly as a 401; an uploaded copy's page reads it as signed out too. A tracker opened before this reads `unknown-game` as no answer and keeps asking, so it drops nothing | Agreed (delegated) | |
