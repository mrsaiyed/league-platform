A first look at the product, with a whole season played in it

These are real pictures of the product as it runs today. Nothing is drawn or mocked. The league in them is made up: 24 schools in 4 divisions, whose season was played from empty to its final tables by one command, through the same screens and rules a real league would use.

Tap any picture to see the whole screen. Most are at a phone's width, because that is where parents and scorekeepers will be.

What is not right yet

You should see these from me before you find them yourself. They are on the list and will be fixed.

  • Standings on a phone. Only games, wins and losses fit. Win percentage and points need a sideways swipe, and the four tables are not the same width.
  • Statistics on a phone. The player table runs off the right edge and needs a swipe.
  • A game's box score in the admin, on a phone. Player names are cut short and the last column is clipped.
  • A team's roster in the admin, on a phone. Each player is a whole form, so the page is very long.
  • A person's record. The box for adding them to another team sits right under their current team and reads as if it were the current one.
  • The home page after a season ends. "The next matchups" shows only the postponed games.
  • Four admin areas say "Coming". Registration, Standings and stats, Site and Messages are not built yet. That is true, not a glitch.

One thing that is the simulator and not the product: in the made-up games every quarter's plays stop with four minutes left. That is how the script spaces its taps.

Trying it with your own hands

Pictures only go so far. The practice site has to run on a computer; the cloud machine that builds it cannot hand out a link.

  • On your own computer. Open the Claude desktop app, share a folder under Settings, Claude Code, Remote Control, and tell the lead in the chat "start the practice site on my computer". The rest is done for you.
  • Online. A practice copy that needs no set-up is the next line of work after the first test.
