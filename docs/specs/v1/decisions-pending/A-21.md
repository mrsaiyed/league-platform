## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| A-21 | How a postponed game is given its new date | In Admin → Games, "Waiting for a new date" (linked from Games and from Schedule), each game has "Give it a new date": date, time and court. Like every change to a published schedule, it goes into the schedule draft, checked for clashes like any edit there, and reaches everyone when the schedule is published; publishing it makes the game scheduled again. Postponing asks with two answers: "postpone it and give it a new date now", which leads straight to that form, or "decide later". "Put it back on its date" stays. Why: one way for every schedule change, so the summary, the clash question and telling people stay the same; the admin can still publish at once |
