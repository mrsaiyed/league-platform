## What changed for this meeting

The concept now uses real AHS court photography, the Lions crest, and an early-to-recent team-history section. Around AHS shows one full-width game-video placement and two half-width highlight placements, followed by archive photos. These are still-photo previews; video hosting, YouTube embedding, uploads, and playback are not implemented.

The presentation copy follows the founder's v4 proposal: $75 registration, a target of 28 players across four teams of seven, grades 9-12 priority with possible grade 8 entry afterward, and a proposed November 2026-February 2027 season on Mondays and Thursdays from 4:00-6:30 p.m. All remain subject to approval. Fictional six-player rosters and sample results illustrate a separate scenario, not actual school registrations.

The earlier specification is still a draft. These presentation edits do not ratify it or add production functionality. External-game statistics, training calendars, and video delivery remain proposed scope. The sample-state key was advanced to v2 so previous $50 demo payments cannot conflict with the new $75 presentation; old local demo history is not reused.
