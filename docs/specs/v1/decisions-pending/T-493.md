## T4 — Keeping leagues apart, sign-in, security

| T-493 | A league's rows never sit in the job queue's schema (step 2.112's review, T-60, T-490). The door T-490 opened in `pgboss` is only for ordinary tables WITHOUT a `league_id` column. A table with one there that the worker's login or a role it reaches may use (it, or `queue_owner`, owning it included) is refused, whatever its row-level security and policies, and the mending ends the ownership without losing a row: the owner grants itself the table owner's role (SET and INHERIT) for the moment, moves the table to `public` and gives it to the migrations' owner, then takes the role back. It never says `revoke select` alone, which its owner could undo, nor `drop`. Proved on the real PostgreSQL: after the printed mending the worker cannot switch the table's fence off or read it, the row is kept, and the start is still refused (a table no migration made, in `public`) until a person decides. | Agreed (delegated) | |
