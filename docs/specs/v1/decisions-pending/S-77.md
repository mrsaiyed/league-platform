| S-77 | What "Enter again" does on a play kept on the scorekeeper's device (one this device recorded that the league never got, or that the league refused) | It is offered only while this device is the one scoring the game. "Enter again" then records the play as a new play now, the same way as a tap (the same checks, and Undo takes it back), and takes it off the list. The clock, Undo, Redo and End game are never entered again. On a device that is not scoring the game the list has no button and says it is for reading out to whoever is. Why: the league's record must have one writer at a time, and a scorekeeper who lost the game for a minute must not lose the plays.
