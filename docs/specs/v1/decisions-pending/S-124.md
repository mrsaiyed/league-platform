## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-124 | The running-clock switch inside the last minutes, and the stop clock a switch On then Off there leaves (step 2.248's fix round) | Inside the last minutes the scorer's running-clock switch stays usable, as S-21 says (the scorer can override anything automatic). The engine has no switch that ends with the last minutes, so a scorer who turns the running clock On and then Off inside them, in a game that was on a running clock before those taps, leaves it on a stop clock afterwards. Until the engine learns that (step 2.252), the scoring screen SAYS SO, in visible words, at the moment it happens and while it is so: 'The clock now stops on fouls for the rest of the game. Tap Running clock to change it back.' Where the game was already on a stop clock (a stop-clock season, or Off chosen before the last minutes), Off only puts back the clock it had and nothing is said. Nothing is locked and nothing is hidden. | The choice that leaves the scorer the most room and is easiest to change |
