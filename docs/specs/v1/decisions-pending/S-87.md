| S-87 | A team that leaves mid-season (the 2.180 review: it stays in public Teams and standings with no mark) | Its played results stay, so other teams' records add up; it stays in the standings, on Teams and on its own page with a short plain mark, "Left the league", and is not offered for new games (the add-game form leaves it out, and a new game naming it is refused with a sentence); its games still to come are the admin's to forfeit or cancel, each, from Games (this week's or Later games). Reason: nothing anyone played is lost, the admin chooses what happens to each game, and a mark set by the admin is taken off as easily. Easy to change: one state on a team. | Small |
