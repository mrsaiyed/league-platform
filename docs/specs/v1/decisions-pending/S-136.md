## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-136 | A player typed in at the scorer's table has no age: how they are shown (step 2.146, the 2.162 finding; amended in its fix and closing rounds) | With no birth date and no tick they count as a child until an admin says otherwise (the tick off or on, or a date), on the roster, statistics, game page, snapshot and live message, in every league. The admin keeps every choice: Admin → People lists them ("Added at the scorer's table, age not recorded"); their record says why they are shown as a child; choosing "An adult", "A child" or a date ends the rule for that person. The parents' confirmation for full names never covers them: they are shown as a child whatever it says, so an adult league whose only children are walk-ups is not offered it. One typed as one word ("Walkington") is shown by its initial only ("W."); one typed in two parts keeps its first name ("Quill U."). A league with one is kept out of search engines, as one with a recorded child is; the publish check still reads recorded children alone. Privacy says in one line that walk-ups are shown as children and keep the site out of search until marked, with a link to the list. They are still not recorded children for the S-109 put-back | The careful reading by default: a child who walks up to the table in an adult league is never shown by full name. The review of step 2.146 found the first version took the league-wide switch from an adult league with no recorded child (R-14, S-21): the list and the record's reason give the admin the way to mark them. The confirming look then found that the parents' confirmation, given for the children the league knows, cannot speak for a walk-up nobody has asked about, that a single typed word may be a surname, and that such a child's name could reach a search engine: hence the closing round. Counting them as recorded children would turn an adult league's confirmed "children in full" back to no in the middle of a game |
