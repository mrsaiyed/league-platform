## T6 — Live stats and realtime

| T-321 | What the device keeps of the tracker belongs to the account that made it. The tracker page carries `<meta name="srleagues-account" content="{account id}">` (also on its refusals); the worker shows a kept page only while the network says that account is signed in, or does not answer within three seconds (with no signal the device is still the maker's: signing in needs the network). When the network says another account or nobody (sent to sign in, refused as signed out, a page that names no account), every kept page of another account is deleted and never shown. A kept page that names no account is never shown. Each play in the queue keeps `accountId`; a game's queue is sent, listed in "Your games" and dismissed only for its maker, so another account's plays stay on the device for their own account and the signed-in one sees none of them. Plays stored before the maker was kept are listed for whoever is signed in. The store of opened games already let go of another account's games on opening (`stillKept`). Rules: `accountMarkOfPage`, `signedInFrom`, `mayServeKeptPage` (tracker-worker-rules.ts), `madeBy` (queue-storage.ts). | Agreed (delegated) | |
