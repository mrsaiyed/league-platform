## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| U-16 | Which offensive words a short name may not be, and how they are matched | A starter list of 81 English words with no everyday meaning a league could want (swearing, slurs, sexual words, Nazi words), in `packages/core/src/modules/leagues/blocked-short-names.ts`. A name is refused when it is one of them or has one as a word between its hyphens (`shit-hoops`), in any capitals; a word that only holds one inside it (`grapevine-hoops`) is taken. It holds the plurals of the words that have them (`fuckers`, `nazis`) and the anti-Muslim slurs the 2.98 review found taken (`raghead`, `towelhead`, `sandnigger`, and their plurals). Words offensive only in some uses (`cock`, `dyke`, `fag`, `spic`), words joined without a hyphen (`shithoops`), misspellings, numbers for letters and other languages are not on it: left for later. The lead approved it as a starter list with the whole-word rule on 2026-11-22; it stays decided for you, for the founder to review. The script that makes a league says which it was: "that word is one of ours (the site uses it for its own pages)" or "that word is not allowed (it is on our list of offensive words)", then "Choose another." Why: a children's league site must not be reachable at such an address, and refusing a word inside a longer name keeps the list short | Add or remove a word: one line in one file. Matching whole names only is a small change in one function |
