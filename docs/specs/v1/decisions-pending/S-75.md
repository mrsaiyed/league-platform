## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-75 | What a game called off can still become | A postponed game may be recorded as a forfeit (a team cannot make any new date), and the screen offers it ("Forfeit or cancel instead"); every other result waits: a score typed, a box score, the tracker and an imported score are all refused until it has a date again, and the import leaves that score waiting and says why. A postponement made by mistake is put back on its old date, with a reason, by whoever may postpone ("Put it back on its date"). A cancelled game put back is what it was when it was cancelled: scheduled, or postponed and waiting. Line-ups already set at the scorer's table are kept. Why: one wrong tap must not lose a game (A-5), and no screen can give a new date until step 2.53 | A small change in one rule (step 2.158) |
