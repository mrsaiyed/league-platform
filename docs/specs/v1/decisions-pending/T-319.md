## T6 — Live stats and realtime

| T-319 | The tracker is installable and reopens with no signal through a worker of its own per league, at `/{league}/score/tracker-worker.js` (address helper kinds `tracker-manifest` and `tracker-worker`), whose reach is that league's tracker addresses. It keeps only a game's page opened by the browser and the built code and two shared stylesheets the page loads, only whole answers from our own address (never a refusal or a sign-in redirect), and signing out deletes all it kept and the worker. Update rule (T-127): while the device's store of opened games holds a game, or the worker cannot tell, a kept page opens at once from the device and is not replaced, and a new worker waits; with none open, the network first and the earlier releases' copies are let go. The rules are plain functions with unit tests, and the worker's script is made from their own text. The manifest and its icons are the same for every league (the tracker's own look) and read nothing of the league; the app opens the game it was added from | Agreed (delegated) | |
