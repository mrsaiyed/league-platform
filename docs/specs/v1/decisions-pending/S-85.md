## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-85 | "Overturn a result" and "Award a game not played" on Admin → Standings and stats (the numbers audit's M6) | Each is one action with one reason, saved as a pair of adjustments, one per team, so the two halves never exist apart (taking either off takes both). Overturn: the admin picks a decided game; its winner gets −1 win +1 loss, its loser +1 win −1 loss; games played and the score stay as they are; the footnote names the game ("Falcons 61–55 Badgers: …"). Award: the admin picks the team it goes to and the team it goes against; a win for one, a loss for the other, and one game played each, so both records add up. Why: the audit's recommendation (N9); games played +1 keeps W + L + T equal to games played. The free adjustment stays beside them, unchanged. A game has one overturn at a time: a second is refused with a sentence, and a game already overturned is not in the form's list (a double tap must not make −2 wins). While a game's result is overturned, reopening it, correcting it or moving it to the bin is refused with one plain sentence that says where to go ("This result was overturned. Take the overturn off in Standings and stats first."): a correction that flipped the winner would otherwise apply the ruling the wrong way round. Taking the overturn off is one tap and stays the admin's choice. Why: the record always adds up, nothing is undone silently, and it is easy to loosen later. Head-to-head tiebreakers still read an overturned game as played (packages/sports is not changed): the overturn changes the two teams' wins and losses only. An award is not linked to a scheduled game: if that game is played later, it counts twice (link it or warn, docs/build/polish.md) | Small |
