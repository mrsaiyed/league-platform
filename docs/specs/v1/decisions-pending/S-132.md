## Decided for you, waiting for your yes

| S-132 | Whether our staff may still remove a league made by mistake that was never published, once it is archived or suspended (U-9 makes such a league read-only; step 2.228's role matrix) | No: only a league still in draft can be removed. An archived or suspended league is refused ("league-in-use"), even one never published: it is taken out of that state first (a suspension is lifted by our staff, which puts the league back to draft; a league only reaches archived after being published, so it is no draft). Why: it is what the product already does (the database refuses any league not in draft), U-9 says a suspended league's data is kept, and removing is the one step that cannot be undone, so it waits for the league to be put back first | Say so and a never-published league can be removed in any state |
