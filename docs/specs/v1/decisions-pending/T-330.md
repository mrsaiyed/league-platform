## T6 — Live stats and realtime

| T-330 | The tracker's rosters and changes of players on the device (step 2.118). A change of players (add, several numbers in one line, renumber, rename, take off) goes through the controller like a tap: timed by the server's time, checked against the contract and against the server's own rules (S-37, S-45) before anything is stored, every change of one line checked before any is stored, stored before it is listed, refused when the device is not scoring, listed as not saved on a loss, in the one queue with the person who made it (T-327). The device fetches the rosters on opening, on taking over, after a loss, after a refused change and when back online, and keeps each. The list is always the kept rosters with this device's waiting changes on top; a change the server took is laid on the kept rosters until a fetch begun after it answers. With no answer on opening the device starts from the rosters it kept before this opening, not the page's copy. An add whose answer was lost is checked against the fetched rosters before it is called not saved, and one found there leaves the list and the storage. | Agreed (delegated) | |
